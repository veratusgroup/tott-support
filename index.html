What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 16.0 or later.
  • Tott is made for iPhone, in portrait.

How to play

  • Each board gives you number tiles and a target.
  • Pick two tiles, then add (+), subtract (−), multiply (×) or divide (÷).
  • The two tiles merge into one new tile. The first tile you pick goes first: 8, then 3, then − makes 5.
  • Keep going until one tile is left. If it equals the target exactly, you have solved the board.
  • Some boards can be solved in more than one way. Each way is a route.

Ways to play

Journey
Chapters of boards, at your own pace. 5 take-backs a board; stuck with none left, start the board again, free.
Daily
One new puzzle a day, the same for everyone.
Gauntlet
Boards one after another, with no clock. 3 undos and 3 hints a run. When you are stuck with no undos left, the run ends.
Timed
Boards against the clock. Time left when you solve a stage carries to the next one. The run ends when the clock reaches 0.
Math Lit
The target is a clue to work out first, such as 3² + 15. Every stage you clear adds a block to your tower. A run has 3 hearts. The only mode where beads buy help.
Pro
The target is an equation to solve, such as 3x − 4 = 68, or a short word problem. A run has 3 hearts, 3 undos and 3 hints. Pro comes with Tott Plus; its coached intro is free.

The Gauntlet, Timed, Math Lit and Pro each have one ranked run a day, the same for everyone, and practice runs.

Questions

When does a new day start?

  • The Daily changes at midnight where you are, on your iPhone’s local time.
  • The ranked runs change at midnight UTC. In the UK that is 1 am in summer.

Do I need an account?

  • No. There is no Tott account, and nothing to sign up for.

Where is my progress saved?

  • On your iPhone only. We never receive it.
  • Deleting Tott deletes your progress. We cannot restore it, because we never hold a copy.
  • Tott Plus and Unlock Everything are safe. Restore purchases gets them back from the App Store.
  • Bought beads are kept on this iPhone. Like your progress, they are lost if Tott is deleted. An iPhone backup keeps them.

Are there ads or tracking?

  • No ads, ever. No analytics and no tracking.
  • Tott collects no personal data. See our Privacy Policy.

What is free, and what is never sold?

  • Tott is a complete game, free to play.
  • Hints, the Daily and every accessibility setting are never sold, and neither is another go at a ranked run.
  • Outside Math Lit, the help is the same for everyone: undo as often as you like in the Daily, 5 take-backs a board in the Journey, and 3 undos and 3 hints a run in the Gauntlet and Pro.
  • Beads are earned by playing, in every mode. Bead packs are sold too.
  • Beads buy help in Math Lit only: an extra undo, a Step, or one more heart in a practice run.
  • In a ranked Math Lit run, Tott asks before your first buy. After it, that run is not sent to the leaderboards.
  • There are no ads and no energy, so there is nothing to pay to remove.
  • Tott Plus (a monthly or yearly subscription), Unlock Everything (a one-time purchase) and bead packs are optional.

I bought something and it is not there.

  • In Tott, tap Restore purchases.
  • It asks the App Store for Tott Plus and Unlock Everything bought with this Apple Account, and puts them back.
  • Beads cannot be restored: the App Store does not restore them.
  • If you paid and it still has not arrived, email us with the date and we will help.

How do I cancel Tott Plus?

  • Open Settings on your iPhone.
  • Tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tott Plus.
  • Tap Cancel Subscription.

How do I ask for a refund?

  • Apple handles refunds.
  • Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can my family share it?

  • Yes. Tott Plus and Unlock Everything work with Family Sharing. Bead packs are not shared.

Something went wrong. How do I report it?

  • In Tott, open Settings, then About, and tap Copy diagnostics.
  • Paste it into an email to us, and tell us what happened.
  • It holds the app version and build, the iOS version, the iPhone model, and the app’s recent notes, errors among them.
  • It holds no name and no identifier. Tott sends nothing by itself.

Accessibility

  • Tott is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface the Braille Institute designed for readers with low vision.
  • If you hit an accessibility problem, please email us. We treat it as a bug, not a feature request.
