Privacy Policy

Tott · Veratus Games · Last updated 27 September 2026

The short version

  • Tott does not collect any personal data.
  • There are no Tott accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your progress is saved on your iPhone, not by us.
  • Leaderboards, the weekly League and achievements use Apple’s Game Center. Purchases use the App Store. Apple runs both. Tott sends nothing to us.

Who we are

  • Tott is made by Veratus Games, a trading name of Veratus Group Limited.
  • Veratus Group Limited is a private limited company, company number 17258282.
  • Registered in England and Wales. Registered office: Office 7258, 58 Peregrine Road, Ilford IG6 3SZ, United Kingdom.
  • Email: sayhello@veratusgames.com

What Tott collects

Nothing. The app:

  • never asks for your name, email address, location or your iPhone’s contacts;
  • never uses your iPhone’s advertising identifier;
  • contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code;
  • shows no tracking prompt, because it does not track you;
  • has no servers of its own, so it sends nothing to us.

What is saved on your iPhone

  • Your progress: Journey, your Daily results and streak, your best in the Gauntlet, Timed, Math Lit and Pro, and the routes you have found.
  • Your settings, including any reminder time.
  • It is kept on your iPhone only. We never receive it.
  • Deleting Tott deletes it. We cannot restore it, because we never hold a copy.

Purchases

  • Tott Plus (a subscription) and Unlock Everything (a one-time purchase) are sold through the App Store.
  • Apple takes the payment. We never see your card details, your name or your Apple Account.
  • Tott uses Apple’s StoreKit, on your iPhone, to ask the App Store what you own.
  • Tott has no server, so the answer is never sent to us.
  • Restore purchases asks the App Store the same question again. Apple’s own system handles it.

Game Center

  • Tott uses Apple’s Game Center for its leaderboards, the weekly League and its achievements.
  • Game Center is optional. Tott is fully playable without it.
  • If you sign in, Apple’s Game Center handles your sign-in, your scores and your friends, under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • To show your friends’ scores, iOS may ask whether Tott can see your Game Center friends. You can say no.
  • What Tott sends to Game Center, only while you are signed in:
    • your scores from ranked runs: Gauntlet boards cleared, Timed stages cleared, and Math Lit and Pro tower points;
    • for the Daily’s League board, the number of routes you found on that week’s Dailies;
    • the achievements you earn.
  • Your League scores are sent only once you have solved 3 Dailies that week.
  • It never sends your streak. Practice runs, Build it, and a Timed run with the Extra time setting never send a score.
  • Tott sends none of this to us. Tott has no server.

Reminders

  • Reminders are off unless you turn them on.
  • Your iPhone schedules them itself. Nothing is sent from a server.

Sharing your result

  • The Share button opens your iPhone’s share sheet with a short text:
    • the puzzle number;
    • how many routes you found, as a row of coloured squares;
    • a link to Tott on the App Store.
  • A finished Math Lit or Pro tower shares a shorter text: the mode, the blocks and the points, with one square per block. It has no link.
  • It never includes the board, your answers or anything about you.
  • You choose where it goes. Tott does not see where you send it.

If you email us

  • We receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to reply to you.
  • In Tott, Settings, then About, then Copy diagnostics copies a short technical note to your clipboard:
    • the app version and build, the save version, the iOS version, the iPhone model, and the app’s recent notes (errors, and problems saving or loading);
    • it contains no name and no identifier. Ids and user names in the notes are blanked out first.
  • The app sends nothing. The note reaches us only if you paste it into an email yourself.

Crash reports

  • Tott contains no crash-reporting code.

Children

  • Tott is made for a general audience.
  • It is not directed at children. It is not designed or marketed for them in particular.
  • Tott collects no personal data from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Your rights

  • UK data protection law (the UK GDPR) gives you rights over your personal data.
  • The app holds none of your personal data, so there is nothing in it for us to show you, correct or delete.
  • If you have emailed us, you can ask to see those emails, or ask us to delete them. Write to sayhello@veratusgames.com.
  • You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
